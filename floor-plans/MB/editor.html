Map edits

Door: click on a drawn (closed) door to open it; click a room-door node to remove or restore its default opening. Wall / Passable: click the corners, Enter to close, Esc to cancel. Passable = furniture people can walk between (seat rows). Shift+click an edit deletes it. Saved with the graph into app/src/main/assets/maps/.

Keys

Wheel = zoom, drag empty space = pan. Del deletes the selection, Esc stops a chain, Ctrl+Z undo, [ ] change floor, C R S E X node type. Stairs and elevators connect between floors through the same vertical group.
